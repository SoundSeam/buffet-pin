## Review cadence

- During implementation: review affected rows with every milestone status update.
- Before sandbox/on-site testing: review all P0/P1 risks and confirm owners.
- During pilot: manager and technical owner review daily, including new incidents and metric trends.
- Expansion: no expansion while a P0 is uncontrolled or a repeated P1 trend lacks a tested corrective action.

Closing a risk requires evidence that probability/impact is acceptably controlled; merely completing code does not close hardware, people, finance, legal, or production risks.
